# Motivation for Choosing IVF Journey Tracker as a Project Topic

## Introduction

The IVF Journey Tracker project addresses a critical healthcare need affecting millions of couples worldwide. Choosing this topic for an academic or professional project is motivated by a combination of social impact, technological innovation, and market opportunity.

---

## 1. Massive Social Impact

### The Infertility Crisis
- **1 in 8 couples** in India struggle with infertility
- **30-40%** of IVF cycles result in live birth globally
- **40%** of IVF patients experience clinical depression
- Failed cycles cause **2x increased risk** of depression

### Emotional & Financial Burden
- Average IVF cycle costs **$12,000-$15,000** in USA
- **₹2-4 lakhs** per cycle in India
- **3-4 cycles** typically needed for success
- **$4.5 billion** spent annually on IVF in India alone
- Stress reduces IVF success rates by **up to 30%**

**Motivation:** This project directly helps couples navigate one of life's most challenging journeys, providing emotional support, financial savings through better success rates, and reducing the mental health burden on patients.
